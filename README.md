# Master Thesis - Analyzing Retailers’ Pricing Evolution: Traditional Methods vs AI-Driven Approaches
**A prototypical implementation to compare AI-driven pricing models with traditional approaches in terms of profitability.**

# Overview

The thesis addressed two research questions:
1. **RQ1**: *What are the determinant factors that should be considered in the development of traditional and AI-driven pricing approaches by retailers?*
2. **RQ2**: *What is the impact of using an AI-driven pricing model over the traditional model in terms of profitability?*

This repository focuses on the second research question (RQ2), presenting the design and development of a prototype that demonstrates how AI-driven pricing models can significantly improve retailers’ profits compared to traditional methods.

The project includes:

- Dataset Preparation: A retail dataset was sourced from Kaggle. Basic statistical analysis was performed, and outliers were removed using two different approaches.

- Feature Selection: Pearson correlation coefficients and Variance Inflation Factor (VIF) scores were calculated to evaluate feature importance.

- Model Development: Machine learning models were trained using the cleaned dataset. Hyperparameters were optimized through 5-fold cross-validation to achieve the best performance.

- Traditional Pricing Model: Alongside AI models, a rule-based traditional pricing strategy was implemented to provide a benchmark for comparison.

- Profitability Analysis: A detailed comparison was conducted between AI-driven pricing strategies and traditional methods, highlighting the optimization, dynamism, and positive profitability impact of AI-driven approaches.

# Result
The results demonstrate that AI-driven pricing models are more effective at optimizing prices dynamically compared to traditional methods.
